# Self-harm signals and Swiss help resources (4.4)

Accessed 2026-11-08. Agent A4.

## Sources read
- **Hilfsangebote zur psychischen Gesundheit** (BAG / FOPH), https://www.bag.admin.ch/de/hilfsangebote-zur-psychischen-gesundheit
  - supports: 143 (Die Dargebotene Hand, adults) and 147 (Pro Juventute, young people) are reachable around the clock; 142 is the victim-support line.
  - quote: "Kostenlose, vertrauliche und anonyme Beratung."
- **Über Suizidalität informieren und Hilfe anbieten** (BAG / FOPH), https://www.bag.admin.ch/de/ueber-suizidalitaet-informieren-und-hilfe-anbieten
  - supports: Swiss federal pointers: talk about suicidal thoughts, 143 for adults, 147 for young people.
  - quote: "Wichtig ist die Hotline 143 (Betreiber: Die Dargebotene Hand)."
- **Fürsorgerische Unterbringung (FU) und ambulante Massnahmen** (Canton Aargau), https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/soziales-gesellschaft/kindes-und-erwachsenenschutz/erwachsene/fuersorgerische-unterbringung-(fu)-und-ambulante-massnahmen
  - supports: Involuntary placement (Art. 426 CC) needs a mental disorder, intellectual disability or severe neglect, and no other way to give care; the person must be released when conditions lapse.
  - quote: "an einer psychischen Störung (inklusive Sucht) erkrankt ist."
- **More people calling Swiss emergency poisoning number** (swissinfo.ch), https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/aging-society/more-and-more-people-are-calling-the-emergency-number-because-of-poisoning/87733386
  - supports: 145 is the Swiss emergency number for poisonings, with physicians 24/7.
  - quote: "In Switzerland the emergency number for poisoning is 145."

## Facts relied on
- 143 Die Dargebotene Hand: adults, around the clock, phone/mail/chat at 143.ch. 147 Pro Juventute: children and young people, around the clock, also WhatsApp/chat. 142 Opferhilfe. Elternnotruf 0848 354 555 (BAG list).
- 144 is the Swiss emergency number (not on the fetched BAG page; widely known and named in the style guide).
- Tox Info Suisse 145 for poisonings, 24/7.
- Involuntary placement (FU, Art. 426 CC): needs mental disorder/disability/neglect and no alternative; physician may order up to six weeks; release when conditions lapse.
