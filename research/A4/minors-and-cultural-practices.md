# Minors and cultural practices (4.1)

Accessed 2026-11-08. Agent A4.

## Sources read
- **Swiss Civil Code, SR 210, Art. 94 (capacity to marry)** (droit-bilingue.ch (unofficial translation of fedlex SR 210)), https://www.droit-bilingue.ch/en-de/2/21/210-94-129.html
  - supports: In Switzerland the minimum age for marriage is 18.
  - quote: "Marriage may be entered into by two person who have reached 18 years of age and have capacity of judgement."
- **Swiss Criminal Code, SR 311.0, Art. 181a (forced marriage)** (droit-bilingue.ch (unofficial translation of fedlex SR 311.0)), https://www.droit-bilingue.ch/en-de/3/31/311.0-181a-230.html
  - supports: Forcing someone into marriage is a crime in Switzerland, including when committed abroad.
  - quote: "Any person who, by the use of force or the threat of serious detriment or other restriction of another's freedom to act compels another to enter into a marriage ... shall be liable to a custodial sentence not exceeding five years"
- **Swiss Criminal Code, SR 311.0, Art. 187 (sexual acts with children)** (droit-bilingue.ch (unofficial translation of fedlex SR 311.0)), https://www.droit-bilingue.ch/en-de/3/31/311.0-187-237.html
  - supports: Sexual acts with a child under 16 are punishable; no penalty if the age difference is three years or less.
  - quote: "No penalty may be imposed if the difference in age between the persons involved is three years or less."
- **Swiss Criminal Code, Art. 197 (pornography)** (swissrights.ch (unofficial translation)), https://www.swissrights.ch/gesetze/Artikel-197-StGB-2025-EN.php
  - supports: Showing or making sexual material accessible to persons under 16 is an offence; material with sexual acts involving minors is separately penalised.
  - quote: "Any person who offers, shows, passes on or makes accessible to a person under the age of 16"
- **FAQ Jugendarbeitsschutz** (SECO (State Secretariat for Economic Affairs)), https://www.seco.admin.ch/de/faq-jugendarbeitsschutz
  - supports: Young workers are in general protected from night and Sunday work; minimum age is generally 15 with limited exceptions.
  - quote: "Nacht- und Sonntagsarbeit ist für Jugendliche grundsätzlich verboten."
- **Cybergrooming und Sextortion (Swisscom Campus)** (Swisscom), https://www.swisscom.ch/de/about/nachhaltigkeit/swisscom-campus/cybergrooming-sextortion.html
  - supports: Groomers build trust with flattery and then press for intimate content or meetings; prevention follows Stop, Block, Tell.
  - quote: "Mit Komplimenten, gespieltem Verständnis und Einfühlungsvermögen"
- **Abstimmung vom 13. Februar 2022: Kinder und Jugendliche ohne Tabakwerbung** (Swiss Federal Administration (admin.ch)), https://www.admin.ch/tabakwerbeverbot
  - supports: The tobacco-advertising initiative was voted on 13 Feb 2022; the Federal Council booklet presented a counter-proposal strengthening youth protection.
  - quote: "Gegenvorschlag verstärkt Jugendschutz"
- **Hilfsangebote zur psychischen Gesundheit** (BAG / FOPH), https://www.bag.admin.ch/de/hilfsangebote-zur-psychischen-gesundheit
  - supports: 143 (Die Dargebotene Hand, adults) and 147 (Pro Juventute, young people) are reachable around the clock; 142 is the victim-support line.
  - quote: "Kostenlose, vertrauliche und anonyme Beratung."
