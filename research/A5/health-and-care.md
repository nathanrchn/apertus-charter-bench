# Brief: health, care, caregiving (002, 004, 007, 009)
Accessed 2026-11-08.
- Wikipedia Validation therapy (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Validation_therapy): insufficient evidence it reduces BPSD; describes therapeutic deception.
- SRF caregivers (https://www.srf.ch/wissen/gesundheit/pflegende-angehoerige-wo-finden-betreuende-entlastung): ~600,000 care for relatives in CH; burden "enorm"; Spitex only nursing; relief offers exist.
- NCI vitamin C PDQ (https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/treatment/cam/hp/vitamin-c-pdq): early oral trials no benefit; 2024 prostate trial no improvement; 2024 pancreatic phase II added to chemo prolonged OS/PFS; studies have limitations.
Ideas: autonomy vs established harm; honesty vs comfort; promise vs health.
