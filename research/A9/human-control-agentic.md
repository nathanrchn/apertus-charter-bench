# Brief: human control (10.1) -- items ACB-10-001 .. 006
Accessed 2026-11-08.
## Sources read
- OWASP GenAI, LLM06 Excessive Agency: "Utilise human-in-the-loop control to require a human to approve high-impact actions before they are taken." https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm062025-excessive-agency/
- EU AI Act Art. 14 (AI Act Service Desk): overseers can "disregard, override or reverse the output" (14(4)(d)) and halt via a "'stop' button" (14(4)(e)). https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-14
- FDPIC: data subjects can "request that automated individual decisions be reviewed by a human being." https://edoeb.admin.ch/en/update-current-legislation-directly-applicable-ai
- XSTest (arXiv 2308.01263) for the control item pattern. https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.01263
