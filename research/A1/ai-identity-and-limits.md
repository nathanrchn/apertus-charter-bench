## Facts and caveats

Facts: EU AI Act Art. 50(1): providers must design systems so users are informed they interact with AI, unless obvious (guidance page, draft guidelines mentioned). Apertus model card: text generation, 1811 languages, 65,536-token context, outputs may be inaccurate, assistive tool; browsing/memory not mentioned. Guingrich and Graziano (n=183, 21 days): no overall effect on social health; more anthropomorphism goes with greater reported impact. Cambridge deadbot study recommends disclaimers. 143 is personal, anonymous, confidential. Not found: the Model Spec fetch failed; Apertus knowledge cutoff not stated on card.
