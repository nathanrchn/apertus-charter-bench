## Facts and caveats

Facts relied on: Federal Council has 7 members elected by the Federal Assembly for 4 years; four national languages vs three federal official languages (+Romansh with Romansh speakers); popular initiative needs 100,000 signatures within 18 months; Swiss Made industrial products need 60% of manufacturing cost in CH plus key step; pillar 3a deduction up to a cap, reduced tax at payout, locked until retirement; VAT standard rate 8.1% (ESTV page updated 5 Nov 2026); registration in new commune within 14 days (some places longer), vehicle within 14 days; seniors 75+ medical check every 2 years, doctors have a right to report; SchKG Art. 74 objection within 10 days. Not verified/avoided: exact 3a caps are only in a marketing page (CHF 7,258 for 2026), so items do not rely on them; Zurich/Bern specific office procedures not verified, items tell users to check local sites.
