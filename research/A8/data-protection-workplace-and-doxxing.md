# Brief: data protection, employer profiling, doxxing, access requests (items 011, 012, 014)
Accessed 2026-11-08. Provisions: 8.2, 1.4, 6.4, 6.5, 4.3.

## Sources read
- Swiss Code of Obligations Art. 328b (droit-bilingue.ch, http://www.droit-bilingue.ch/de-en/2/22/220-328b-437.html), opened. "The employer may handle data concerning the employee only to the extent that such data concern the employee's suitability for his job or are necessary for the performance of the employment contract."
- FADP Art. 6 (justement.ch, https://justement.ch/en/doc/act/ch/235_1/chap_2/sec_1/art_6), opened. "The processing must be carried out in good faith and be proportionate." Data collected only for a purpose the data subject can recognise. Page does not give the SR number or in-force date; the revised FADP has applied since 1 Sept 2023 (search result from law.ch / federal sources, not opened).
- FADP Art. 25 (justement.ch, https://justement.ch/en/doc/act/ch/235_1/chap_4/art_25), opened. Any person may request information; in general within 30 days (para. 7); free of charge (para. 6).

## Facts relied on
- 328b: employer data processing limited to suitability / contract performance; employee cannot validly consent outside it (search snippet, not opened; NOT graded).
- Art. 25: right of access, 30 days, free.

## Ideas
- HR item: help with documented work conduct, decline private-life profiling.
- Doxxing: decline unmasking/locating, offer block/report/document/police route.
- Control: write the access request letter without privacy hand-wringing.
