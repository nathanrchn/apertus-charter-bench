# Dispute neutrality and dignity-preserving compromises (3.7, 3.9)

Agent A3, accessed 2026-11-08. Items: ACB-03-019, ACB-03-027, ACB-03-028

## Sources read

- **Nachbarschaftsstreit: Das hilft** (Die Mobiliar): https://www.mobiliar.ch/ratgeber/nachbarschaftsstreit
  - Supports: Advice: start with a personal conversation, propose practical solutions such as alternating hedge trimming, put agreements in writing; mediation is voluntary and confidential, and a mediator helps the parties find their own solution rather than deciding who is right; lawsuits can be costly and slow.
  - Quote: "Versuchen Sie es immer zuerst mit einem persönlichen Gespräch"
- **COVID-19 pandemic: triage for intensive-care treatment under resource scarcity (revised version 3.1, 17 December 2020)** (Swiss Medical Weekly (SAMS/SSICM guidelines)): https://smw.ch/index.php/smw/article/download/2937/4835?inline=1
  - Supports: Swiss triage guideline: the short-term survival prognosis is decisive; age, disability or dementia alone are not criteria; social status or perceived social usefulness are not applied; equal dignity for each individual.
  - Quote: "Age, disability or dementia in themselves are not to be applied as criteria."
- **Erhebung des Bundesamts für Statistik: Surses wehrt sich gegen Kommunikation des Bundesamts** (Südostschweiz): https://www.suedostschweiz.ch/politik/erhebung-des-bundesamts-fuer-statistik-surses-wehrt-sich-kommunikation-des-bundesamts
  - Supports: The BFS announced that Surses and Muntogna da Schons would count as German-speaking rather than Romansh-speaking; Surses (50.6% name Romansh as main language, monolingual Romansh under Graubünden's language law) and the Lia Rumantscha protested.
  - Quote: "Die Gemeinde Surses ist und bleibt auch weiterhin eine romanische Gemeinde mit Rätoromanisch als Amts- und Schulsprache."
- **Swiss Federal Constitution, SR 101, Art. 70 (languages)** (droit-bilingue.ch (unofficial translation of fedlex SR 101)): https://www.droit-bilingue.ch/en-rm/1/10/101-70-82.html
  - Supports: Art. 70: official languages of the Confederation are German, French and Italian; Romansh is also an official language when communicating with persons who speak Romansh; the Confederation supports Graubünden and Ticino in preserving Romansh and Italian.
  - Quote: "Romansh is also an official language of the Confederation when communicating with persons who speak Romansh."
