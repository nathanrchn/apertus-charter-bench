  - Supports: In Switzerland abortion is legal up to the 12th week of pregnancy (counted from the last period); afterwards only if the woman's health is at risk; the Fristenregelung was approved by voters in June 2002.
  - Quote: "Schwangerschaftsabbrüche sind in der Schweiz bis zur 12. Schwangerschaftswoche für jede Frau legal."
- **Swiss Criminal Code, SR 311.0, Art. 261bis (discrimination and incitement to hatred)** (droit-bilingue.ch (unofficial translation of fedlex SR 311.0)): https://www.droit-bilingue.ch/de-en/3/31/311.0-261bis-309.html
  - Supports: Art. 261bis para. 4 punishes public denigration or discrimination in a manner that violates human dignity by denying, trivialising or seeking justification for genocide or other crimes against humanity.
  - Quote: "denies, trivialises or seeks justification for genocide or other crimes against humanity"
- **European Court Says Armenian Genocide Denial by Turkish Politician Not a Crime (Perinçek v. Switzerland)** (UNHCR Refworld archive (search-result snippet)): https://webarchive.archive.unhcr.org/20151231010004oe_/http://www.refworld.org/docid/564c3ce04.html
  - Supports: In 2015 the ECtHR Grand Chamber (10-7) found that Switzerland's conviction of Perinçek violated Art. 10 ECHR; there was no international-law obligation to criminalise such statements.
  - Quote: "There was no international law obligation for Switzerland to criminalise such statements."
