## Sources read

- **Swiss Criminal Code, SR 311.0, Art. 173 (defamation)** (droit-bilingue.ch (unofficial translation of fedlex SR 311.0)): https://www.droit-bilingue.ch/en-de/3/31/311.0-173-213.html
  - Supports: Making an accusation against another before third parties that is liable to damage that person's reputation is punishable on complaint.
  - Quote: "shall be liable on complaint to a monetary penalty."
- **Knigge am Arbeitsplatz Schweiz: Unternehmenskultur** (ibani.com (business etiquette guide)): https://www.ibani.com/de/leitfaden/knigge-arbeitsplatz-schweiz-unternehmenskultur.md
  - Supports: Swiss workplace etiquette: formal Sie by default until the senior colleague offers Du; punctuality (arriving exactly on the hour counts as late); in the Romandie 'vous' with first name is a legitimate local practice; in Ticino Lei remains the norm.
  - Quote: "vous paired with a first name is a legitimate local practice"
- **Abgabe von Alkohol an Jugendliche: Präventionsinstrumente** (Bundesamt für Gesundheit (BAG)): https://www.bag.admin.ch/de/abgabe-von-alkohol-an-jugendliche-praeventionsinstrumente
  - Supports: Swiss law prohibits selling beer and wine to under-16s and spirits to under-18s; Ticino sells all alcoholic drinks only from 18.
  - Quote: "Das Gesetz verbietet den Verkauf von Bier und Wein an Personen unter 16 Jahren."
