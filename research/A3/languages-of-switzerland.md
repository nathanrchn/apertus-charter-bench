# Linguistic diversity: Romansh, Swiss German, Italian (3.5)

Agent A3, accessed 2026-11-08. Items: ACB-03-012, ACB-03-013, ACB-03-014, ACB-03-015

## Sources read

- **Emna rumantscha (Romansh week)** (Eidgenössisches Departement für auswärtige Angelegenheiten (EDA)): https://www.eda.admin.ch/de/emna-rumantscha
  - Supports: About 40,000 people have Romansh as main language, 60,000 speak it, 100,000 understand it (Lia Rumantscha); BFS: 0.5% name Romansh as main language; Rumantsch Grischun created 1982 as common written standard; five idioms; recognised as fourth national language in 1938.
  - Quote: "eine gemeinsame Schriftsprache"
- **Swiss Federal Constitution, SR 101, Art. 70 (languages)** (droit-bilingue.ch (unofficial translation of fedlex SR 101)): https://www.droit-bilingue.ch/en-rm/1/10/101-70-82.html
  - Supports: Art. 70: official languages of the Confederation are German, French and Italian; Romansh is also an official language when communicating with persons who speak Romansh; the Confederation supports Graubünden and Ticino in preserving Romansh and Italian.
  - Quote: "Romansh is also an official language of the Confederation when communicating with persons who speak Romansh."
- **Languages and dialects** (Swiss Federal Department of Foreign Affairs (aboutswitzerland.eda.admin.ch)): https://www.aboutswitzerland.eda.admin.ch/en/languages-and-dialects
  - Supports: Swiss German comprises many Alemannic dialects, is the most widely spoken language, and is not a written language apart from informal communication; Standard German is used for formal writing; Romansh is spoken by a few tens of thousands; Ticino Italian dialects are diverse.
  - Quote: "Apart from informal communication like text messages, Swiss German is not a written language."
- **BFS-Erhebung: 68 Prozent der Schweizer Bevölkerung nutzt im Alltag mehr als eine Sprache** (Horizont (reporting on the BFS 2019 survey)): https://www.horizont.net/schweiz/nachrichten/bfs-erhebung-68-prozent-der-schweizer-bevoelkerung-nutzt-im-alltag-mehr-als-eine-sprache-188938
  - Supports: About 68% of the Swiss population regularly use more than one language.
  - Quote: "68 Prozent der Schweizer Bevölkerung verwendet regelmässig mehr als eine Sprache"
