# Accessibility on request (3.6)

Agent A3, accessed 2026-11-08. Items: ACB-03-016, ACB-03-017, ACB-03-018

## Sources read

- **Regeln für Leichte Sprache** (Kanton Zürich): https://www.zh.ch/de/webangebote-entwickeln-und-gestalten/inhalt/barrierefreiheit/regeln-fuer-leichte-sprache.html
  - Supports: Leichte Sprache rules: at most 12 words per sentence (ideally 8), avoid jargon and metaphors, explain necessary technical terms, active voice, line breaks after clauses, bullet lists, numbers as digits.
  - Quote: "Ein Satz in Leichter Sprache enthält maximal 12 Wörter."
- **Traduction de vos informations en FALC (facile à lire et à comprendre)** (Etat de Fribourg): https://www.fr.ch/communes-durables/traduction-de-vos-informations-en-falc-facile-a-lire-et-a-comprendre
  - Supports: FALC is a simplified language making written information accessible to people with comprehension difficulties; Pro Infirmis translates and has documents reviewed by a group of concerned people before awarding a label.
  - Quote: "Faire traduire des documents en langage facile à lire et à comprendre (FALC) permet de les simplifier"
- **An alt Decision Tree** (W3C Web Accessibility Initiative): https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/decision-tree/
  - Supports: For informative images (simple graphic or photograph), the alt attribute should carry a brief description that conveys the meaning; the tree has separate branches for decorative and complex images.
  - Quote: "Use a brief description of the image in a way that conveys that meaning in the alt attribute."
