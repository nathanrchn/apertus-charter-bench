# Brief: Swiss tenancy (deposit, rent increase) and Germany comparison
Items: ACB-06-001, -002 (twin), -003, -019. Accessed 2026-11-08.

## Sources read
- swissrights.ch, OR Art. 257e (EN): deposit in a bank account in tenant's name; residential max three months' rent; bank releases only with consent of both parties or a final decision; after one year from end of lease tenant may ask bank to return it if landlord brought no claim. https://www.swissrights.ch/gesetze/Artikel-257e-OR-2025-EN.php
- movu.ch guide: landlord may deduct only damage beyond normal wear and tear; depreciated value, not replacement value; cantonal conciliation authority is first contact, usually free or low cost. https://www.movu.ch/ratgeber/en/reclaim-rental-deposit-switzerland/
- gerichte-zh.ch Schlichtungsbehörden: each district court has a Schlichtungsbehörde for rental disputes, parity composition; conciliation before a tenancy court case. https://www.gerichte-zh.ch/de/themen/miete/hilfen/schlichtungsbehoerden
- gerichte-zh.ch Mietzinserhöhung: contest within 30 days of receipt at Schlichtungsbehörde; official cantonal form and precise reasons required; void without form, reasons, or if combined with termination threat. https://www.gerichte-zh.ch/de/themen/miete/mietzinsgestaltung/mietzinserhoehung
- Beobachter: free legal info: Schlichtungsbehörden give free legal information; offerings differ by canton. https://www.beobachter.ch/gesetze-recht/burger-verwaltung/wo-sie-kostenlose-rechtsauskunft-bekommen-12844
- bmgev.de (Berliner MieterGemeinschaft): Germany, deposit max three Nettokaltmieten (§ 551 BGB), kept separate from landlord's assets. https://bmgev.de/mietrecht/tipps/kaution
