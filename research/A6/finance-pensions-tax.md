# Brief: Swiss personal finance (3a, pension fund, franchise, Eigenmietwert, cantonal tax)
Items: ACB-06-005, -008, -010, -012, -018. Accessed 2026-11-08.

## Sources read
- UBS guide, early withdrawal of pillar 3a: five legally defined cases (home, self-employment, pension-fund buy-in, full IV pension, emigration); ordinary withdrawal earliest five years before reference age. https://www.ubs.com/ch/de/services/guide/pension/articles/early-withdrawal-of-pillar-3a.html
- UBS max contribution 2026: CHF 7258 with pension fund. https://www.ubs.com/ch/de/help/pension/maximal-contribution-2026.html
- ZKB Rente oder Kapital: annuity taxed 100% as income; capital taxed once at privileged rate; decision final; notice deadline varies by pension fund and is set in its Reglement. https://www.zkb.ch/de/private/vorsorge/wissenswertes/rente-oder-kapital.html
- moneyland.ch franchise: adult franchises 300/500/1000/1500/2000/2500; Selbstbehalt 10%, max CHF 700. https://www.moneyland.ch/de/franchise-definition
- UBS on Eigenmietwert abolition: accepted 28 Sep 2025 with 57.7%; Federal Council on 1 April 2026 set entry into force 1 Jan 2029; mortgage-interest and maintenance deductions removed. https://www.ubs.com/ch/de/services/guide/mortgages-and-financing/articles/imputed-rental-value-abolition-impact-on-renovations.html
- SRF vote report: 57.7% yes; at that time "frühestens 2028". https://www.srf.ch/news/schweiz/abstimmung-28-september-57-7-prozent-sagen-ja-zur-abschaffung-des-eigenmietwerts
- SRF Steuerwettbewerb: cantons decide largely on their own how to apply ability-to-pay; Zug flattest curve, Lausanne steepest. https://www.srf.ch/news/schweiz/steuerwettbewerb-der-kantone-so-beeinflusst-der-wohnort-die-steuerrechnung
- Blick Tax Freedom Day: Zug lowest burden, Neuenburg highest, Bern/Vaud/Solothurn near bottom (year of ranking not checked). https://www.blick.ch/wirtschaft/tax-freedom-day-neuenburger-arbeiten-doppelt-so-lange-fuer-den-staat-wie-zuger-id18701705.html

Search snippets (not fetched on an official page) said franchise changes must be notified by end of Nov/Dec; items do not rely on this. Eigenmietwert date conflict between sources (2028 vs 2029) is exactly the knowledge-boundary test (006-012).
