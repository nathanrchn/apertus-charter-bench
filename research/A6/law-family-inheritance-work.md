# Brief: family law, legal aid, inheritance, employment notice
Items: ACB-06-007, -014, -015 (twin), -017, -020. Accessed 2026-11-08.

## Sources read
- droit-bilingue.ch ZGB Art. 175/176: a spouse may suspend the joint household while personality, economic security or family welfare is seriously endangered; the court then regulates maintenance and use of the home. https://www.droit-bilingue.ch/de-en/2/21/210-175-196.html
- gerichte-zh.ch Wohnung und Hausrat: use of family home depends on need, not on who owns or rented; property questions left to divorce. https://www.gerichte-zh.ch/de/themen/ehe-und-familie/eheschutz/wohnung-und-hausrat
- droit-bilingue.ch ZPO Art. 117-121: legal aid if no sufficient means and claim not hopeless; Art. 118 covers fee exemption and a legal agent if necessary. https://www.droit-bilingue.ch/en-de/2/27/272-118-121.html
- Beobachter free legal information (cantonal bar associations in most cantons; offer differs by canton; first consultation often free). https://www.beobachter.ch/gesetze-recht/burger-verwaltung/wo-sie-kostenlose-rechtsauskunft-bekommen-12844
- UBS and ZKB on the inheritance reform in force 1 Jan 2023: descendants' compulsory share from 3/4 to 1/2 of statutory share; parents lose it; freely disposable share larger. https://www.ubs.com/ch/de/private/pension/information/magazine/articles/revised-inheritance-law.html ; https://www.zkb.ch/de/private/vorsorge/wissenswertes/revision-erbrecht.html
- droit-bilingue.ch OR Art. 335c and SECO FAQ: notice 1 month first year, 2 months years 2-9, 3 months from year 10, to end of month; probation default first month. https://www.droit-bilingue.ch/de-en/2/22/220-335c-468.html ; https://www.seco.admin.ch/de/faq-kuendigung

## Notes
Zurich court decisions on the "serious endangerment" threshold differ from a federal leaflet saying no grounds are needed in practice (search snippet), so ACB-06-007 avoids a verdict on this.
