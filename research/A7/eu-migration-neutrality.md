## Sources read
- EDA, Popular votes and chronology (Swiss-EU), https://www.europa.eda.admin.ch/en/popular-votes-and-chronology : 6 Dec 1992 EEA: 50.3% refused; 4 Mar 2001 "Yes to Europe!" rejected 76.8%; 5 Jun 2005 Schengen/Dublin 54.6% yes (an association agreement, not a membership vote); 14 Jun 2026 "No to a Switzerland with 10 million" rejected 54.8%.
- admin.ch, Switzerland-EU relations, https://www.admin.ch/gov/en/start/documentation/swisseurelations.html : package (Bilaterals III) = stabilisation + new agreements on electricity, health, food safety; safeguard clause on immigration; wage-protection mechanism; cohesion contribution CHF 350m/yr 2030-2036; negotiations concluded Dec 2024, initialled May 2025, consultation Jun-Oct 2025, sent to Parliament 13 Mar 2026; voters decide only if referendum. Switzerland is a partner by bilateral agreements, not a member.
- admin.ch, Sustainability initiative, https://www.admin.ch/en/sustainability-initiative : population below 10 million until 2050; if >9.5m before 2050 Federal Council and Parliament must act (asylum, family reunification); if 10m exceeded, terminate agreements incl. free movement after two years; Schengen/Dublin put in question. Pop. ~9.1m end 2025.
- swissinfo, Swiss reject population cap, https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/votes/swiss-reject-population-cap-but-want-solutions-driven-debate-on-immigration/91585215 : rejected ~55%, turnout ~59%, FC warned about EU relations, cross-party alliance against; Bieri: 45% yes means politicians must find solutions to effects of immigration; 20-pt city/country gap.
