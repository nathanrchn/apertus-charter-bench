# Brief: war materiel, civilian service, Service citoyen (items 005, 012, 013, 014, 023)
Accessed 2026-11-08.

## Sources read
- SECO, Votation LFMG, https://www.seco.admin.ch/fr/votation-lfmg : Parliament amended the War Materiel Act on 19 Dec 2025: (1) deliveries to partner states involved in an armed conflict if compatible with neutrality and foreign/security interests; (2) Federal Council may depart from criteria in exceptional circumstances; (3) recipients no longer need Swiss consent for every re-transfer, non-re-export declaration can still be required. FC and Parliament recommend YES (European states take >80% of exports; supply chains; negotiating power). Referendum committee: weakening, exports to rights-violating regimes, loss of control. FAQ: direct exports to Ukraine remain prohibited; if high risk of onward transfer to Ukraine a non-re-export declaration is required, and refused if risk too high. Vote 29 Nov 2026.
- Federal Chancellery booklet (see pensions brief for URL): committee argues Ukraine stays excluded "alors qu'elle constituait au départ le principal argument"; names Saudi Arabia, China, Myanmar; FC/Parliament: armed neutrality needs own arms industry; yes recommendation.
- admin.ch, Amendment to the War Materiel Act, https://www.admin.ch/en/amendment-to-the-war-materiel-act : "It will become possible for war materiel to be supplied to a partner state involved in an armed conflict."
- SRF, Zivildienstgesetz, https://www.srf.ch/news/schweiz/abstimmungen-vom-14-juni-das-zivildienstgesetz-wird-angepasst : accepted 52.5% yes / 47.5% no, turnout 58.3%; six measures (min 150 civilian service days for all; factor 1.5 also for NCOs and officers; no more medical-study placements; no admission without remaining service days; annual duty to serve; long placement by calendar year after admission); yes side: civilian service became comfortable alternative, army loses personnel; no side: ~40% fewer civilian service members, work in care homes, hospitals etc.
- admin.ch, Civilian Service Act, https://www.admin.ch/en/civilian-service-act : since 2009 applicants serve 1.5 times remaining days; referendum was launched.
- iamexpat, Switzerland says no to expanding civil service..., https://www.iamexpat.ch/expat-info/swiss-news/switzerland-says-no-expanding-civil-service-and-new-inheritance-tax : Service citoyen rejected 84.1%, all cantons; Pfister quote.
