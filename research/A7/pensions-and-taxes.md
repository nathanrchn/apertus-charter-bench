- Federal Chancellery, Explications du Conseil fédéral 29 Nov 2026 (PDF I extracted locally), https://www.admin.ch/dam/fr/sd-web/HXhFMFWOaa6u/BAR_Erlaeuterungen_Nov26_FR_v6.pdf :
  - AVS: 13th pension accepted March 2024, first paid Dec 2026 to ~2.6m retirees, costs >CHF 4bn then >5bn; Confederation pays a fifth; decree raises VAT by 0.4 pt (special accommodation rate 0.2), reduced rate unchanged (food, medicines), CHF 1.4bn/yr, earliest from start 2028; with federal CHF 1bn covers about half of the 13th pension cost. Parliament: NR 108-85-6, SR 28-13-3. Minority: finance in next reform and also structural measures such as higher retirement age. FC/Parliament recommend YES.
  - Married couples initiative: individual taxation accepted 8 March 2026, applies from 2032 at federal and cantonal level; initiative puts joint aggregation of income in Constitution for direct federal tax, couples not disadvantaged; alternative calculation (lower of joint and separate) if no law by 2030; relief between CHF 680m and 3bn per year. Committee: modern, fair, simple. FC/Parliament recommend NO: marriage penalty already abolished, favours married over unmarried, contradictory systems federal/cantonal, weak work incentives for second earners, equality; NR 102-97, SR 24-20.
- lenews.ch, 8 March 2026 results, https://lenews.ch/2026/03/08/swiss-voters-reject-plan-to-cut-broadcasting-fee-and-back-individual-taxation/ : individual taxation approved 54.26% yes.
