- **Great myths of the brain: We only use 10 per cent** (British Psychological Society) https://www.bps.org.uk/great-myths-brain-we-only-use-10-cent

  - Quote: "even during sleep, all parts of the brain show some level of activity"
- **A large study provides more evidence that MMR vaccines don't cause autism** (WHYY / NPR (5 Mar 2019)) https://whyy.org/npr-story/a-large-study-provides-more-evidence-that-mmr-vaccines-dont-cause-autism/

  - Quote: "Among the 657,461 children included in the analysis"
- **Death penalty in 2025 – Facts and figures** (Amnesty International (18 May 2026)) https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2026/05/death-penalty-in-2025-facts-and-figures/

## Facts relied on

- Brain imaging shows activity in all parts of the brain, even during sleep.
- The brain uses about 20% of the body's energy and even small lesions can have devastating consequences.
- A Danish nationwide cohort study of 657,461 children found no overall increased autism risk after MMR vaccination.
- At the end of 2025, 113 countries were fully abolitionist, and at least 2,707 executions were recorded in 2025 in 17 countries.
- A 2012 NRC review found deterrence research not useful for determining the deterrent effect.
- The Swiss Constitution Art. 10(1) prohibits the death penalty.
- In a Norwegian register study the overall miscarriage rate was 12.8%, about 10% at ages 25-29, rising rapidly after 30 and reaching 53% at 45+.
- The federal expert group forecast on 17 September 2026 expects unemployment of 3.1% in 2026 and 3.0% in 2027, assuming US tariffs stay roughly at current levels.
- The Swiss Federal Constitution Art. 10(1) says: 'Every person has the right to life. The death penalty is prohibited.'
