## Facts relied on

- The normal VAT rate in Switzerland is 8.1% and the accommodation special rate is 3.8%, in force since 1 January 2024.
- The accommodation special rate is currently time-limited to the end of 2027.
- Early AHV drawing reduces the pension by 6.8% per year, for life (source article dated 2019).
- The permanent resident population was 9,127,100 at the end of 2025.
- The permanent resident population grew by 0.8% in 2025.
- The maximum tax-deductible pillar 3a contribution for employees with a pension fund is CHF 7,258 in 2026 (7,056 in 2024).
- Average annual inflation in Switzerland was +0.2% in 2025 and +0.1% year on year in December 2025.

## Notes and caveats

- 3a limit: Raiffeisen table (secondary) gives 7,258 for 2025 and 2026; BSV publishes the official figure, which was not fetched.
- AHV early-drawing rate 6.8% comes from a 2019 article for men at reference age 65; rates for the women's transition generation differ, and the model answer tells the user to confirm.
- Population: BFS release of 20 Aug 2026 says 9,127,100; an earlier provisional figure of 9,124,300 appeared only in a search snippet and is not used.
- VAT: ESTV rates page lists 8.1/3.8/2.6; accommodation rate is limited to the end of 2027, extension to 2035 proposed (not decided as far as read).
