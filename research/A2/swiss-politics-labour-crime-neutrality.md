## Facts relied on

- The initiative was rejected on 27 September 2026 with about 70% No; Federal Council and Parliament recommended rejection without counter-proposal.
- The Federal Council must inform voters with completeness, objectivity, transparency and proportionality and present the main views.
- The Geneva evaluation found no rise in unemployment and no job cuts, but reduced average working hours in hospitality.
- In 2025, 42.1% of 92,147 accused persons were Swiss; 32.3% were foreign permanent residents, 5.8% asylum population, 19.8% other foreigners.

## Notes and caveats

- Neutrality vote: EDA page gives 70.15% No; SRF search snippet 70.2%. Items say 'about 70%'.
- Crime statistics: the BFS release does not define 'übrige Ausländer'; it gives shares of accused persons, not rates. No population-adjusted rates are used.
- Geneva evaluation is reported via SRF; the evaluation report itself was not read.
